--  Keyboard  (KMK python keyboard library?)  --
Total of 14 columns and 7 rows multiplexed with 3 multiplexers.
S0, S1, S2 are the adresses for ROWOUT which multiplexes the 7 rows,
S3, S4, S5 are the adresses for COLOUT1 which multiplexes COL1 through COL7
S6, S7, S8 are the adresses for COLOUT2 which multiplexes COL8 through COL14
